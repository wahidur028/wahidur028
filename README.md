**AI Researcher | Telco-Expert | Technologist**


I'm a doctoral researcher in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the [Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST)](https://www.gist.ac.kr/en/), exploring the world of Generative AI. My work focuses on pushing the boundaries of open-source language models — from fine-tuning and prompt engineering all the way to deploying real-world NLP/NLU systems that make a difference.

Before academia, I spent over a decade in the telecommunications industry, solving complex problems at scale. Now I bridge that experience with research, building tools and methods that bring AI out of the lab and into production.

I’m passionate about:

- Enhancing the performance of open-source AI frameworks  
- Building scalable NLP/NLU systems  
- Bridging the gap between academia and industry through collaborative R&D  
- Driving innovation in AI-powered solutions for real-world challenges

I actively seek **interdisciplinary collaborations** that foster open innovation and deliver meaningful impact across sectors.
